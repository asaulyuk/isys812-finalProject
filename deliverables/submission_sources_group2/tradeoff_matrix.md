# Tradeoff Matrix and Failure Modes

## Startup (Track A) vs Enterprise (Track B)

| Decision Axis | Track A Upside | Track A Risk | Track B Upside | Track B Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Speed vs Robustness | Fast release and early signal capture | Process debt and rework | Stable operations and controls | Slower value realization | Time-box Track A architecture debt cleanup every sprint |
| Narrow Wedge vs Broad Platform | Clear focus and faster product-market tests | Misses adjacent pain clusters | Multi-journey coverage and reuse | Overbuild before demand proof | Stage-gate expansion only after KPI thresholds |
| Aggressive Positioning vs Reputational Safety | Strong differentiation narrative | Overclaim risk if results drift | Safer external claims and governance | Lower market urgency | Confidence labels on all claims |
| Short-Term Traction vs Long-Term Trust | Quick wins on adoption and cycle time | Unsustained improvements | Durable trust and board-level defensibility | Complexity and change fatigue | Maintain shared KPI spine across both tracks |
| Low Overhead vs High Governance | Lean execution | Control gaps, inconsistent handling | Auditability, policy compliance | Operational overhead | Keep minimum viable control set in Track A |

## Failure Modes to Watch
1. **Feature bloat in Track A** before proving one wedge metric.
2. **Metric gaming** (improving one KPI while worsening recurrence/quality).
3. **One-size-fits-all workflow** that ignores fraud/scam specialized path.
4. **Narrative drift** between predictive evidence and GTM claims.
5. **Premature enterprise rollout** before operational baseline is stable.
