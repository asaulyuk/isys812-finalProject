# Executive One-Pager: Prescriptive Actions From Predictive Signals

## Decision
Execute a dual-track prescriptive strategy with one primary path:
- **Primary (Plan A):** Startup strategy, 0-3 months, focused wedge execution
- **Fallback (Plan B):** Enterprise strategy, 12+ months, activated only if trigger thresholds fail

## Why This Is the Recommended Path
- Predictive outputs consistently indicate concentrated complaint friction in billing/fees, disputes, delinquency/account-status transitions, and lifecycle moments.
- Signal concentration by product line supports targeted interventions, not broad generic fixes.
- Validation outputs indicate stable ranking performance in the current dataset; recommendations are suitable for decision support with explicit confidence guardrails.

## Feature Priorities
1. Transparent Billing Explainer
2. Dispute Fast-Lane
3. Lifecycle Guardrails
4. Fraud/Scam Specialized Workflow (priority rises under Plan B)

## Go-to-Market Priorities
- Position around clarity, speed of resolution, and control.
- Start with high-friction product lines, prove measurable reduction in escalation proxies, then expand.

## KPI Spine (Both Tracks)
- Escalation proxy rate (down)
- Resolution cycle time (down)
- Repeat complaint rate (down)
- Feature adoption in target segment (up)
- Governance incident rate (down; Plan B emphasis)

## Tradeoff Position
- Plan A maximizes speed and differentiation but carries process and control risk.
- Plan B maximizes resilience and governance but slows immediate market motion.
- Recommended posture: run Plan A by default; activate Plan B only via trigger rules.

## Trigger Rule Summary
Move from Plan A to Plan B when 2+ trigger failures persist for 6-8 weeks:
- adoption stall,
- no escalation improvement,
- cycle-time miss,
- recurrence non-improvement,
- governance incident increase,
- economics deterioration.

## Confidence Labels
- **High confidence now:** theme-level prioritization and sequencing.
- **Test-required:** causal claims on financial outcomes or transfer to other channels/datasets.
