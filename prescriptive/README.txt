prescriptive/
=============

Actions, playbooks, and recommendations derived from predictive escalation model outputs.
Organized in two layers: strategic (where/how to compete) and operational (what to build/measure).

Strategic prescriptive artifacts
---------------------------------
opportunity_themes.md
  Pain clusters extracted from predictive signals. Foundation for all product and GTM decisions.

feature_and_gtm_strategy.md  [Strategic Layer section]
  Track A vs Track B market strategy, GTM positioning, segment targeting, and proof metrics.

tradeoff_matrix.md
  Explicit tradeoffs and failure modes for Track A vs Track B selection.

fintech_competitive_slice.md
  Fintech-focused CFPB cohort analysis (PayPal/Venmo, Square/Block-side) vs large-bank baseline.
  Informs positioning differentiation.

executive_one_pager.md
  Decision-level summary for stakeholder communication.

Operational prescriptive artifacts
------------------------------------
feature_and_gtm_strategy.md  [Operational Layer section]
  Feature scopes, KPI targets, and execution specs for each initiative.

fallback_trigger_rules.md
  Specific metric thresholds and reporting cadence. Triggers strategic track change if 2+ fail
  for 6-8 consecutive weeks.

prioritized_backlog.csv
  Ranked action backlog with owner roles, KPI targets, timelines, and dependencies.

Framework overview
-------------------
prescriptive_strategic_gap_analysis.md
  Explains the two-tier framework and the analytical gap this project closes.
